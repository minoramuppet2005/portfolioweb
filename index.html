Hi! My name is Miranda

I'm a graduate student at Florida State University studying Information Technology with a focus on user-centered design, and a passion for creativity, visual communication, and human-centered design.

I've always been drawn to the space where creativity and technology meet. I love exploring how thoughtful design can make information more engaging, approachable, and meaningful. Whether that means experimenting with a new visual style or thinking through how people interact with something, I enjoy finding creative ways to communicate an idea.

Outside of school, I enjoy learning new things, getting involved in my community, and finding new outlets for creativity. I'm someone who is always creating and learning!

My work has impact

For the past two years, I have served as the Social Media Director for the Askew Student Life Center (ASLC) at FSU, where I shape the digital voice behind a space that brings students together through film, gaming, and themed events.

My marketing has helped drive attendance for several sold-out events in the ASLC's 380-seat theater. My work ranges from large-scale campaigns like an advanced screening of Wicked: For Good (2025), where I worked closely with NBC Universal professionals, to collaborations with organizations across the university.

Through visual design, strategic promotion, real-time coverage, and consistent storytelling, I manage a fast-moving content calendar of 10-25 posts each week and grow the ASLC's Instagram community.

Growing ASLC's digital presence

When I took over ASLC's social media in 2025, our Instagram had around 10K followers. Since then, I've grown the account to over 15K while developing a stronger visual identity and creating content designed to connect students with our events, films, and programs.

What I'm most proud of isn't just the growth. It's learning what makes our audience stop, engage, and come back. From original graphics to short-form video and trend-based content, I've experimented creatively while seeing that work translate into measurable results.
